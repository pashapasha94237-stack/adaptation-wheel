Wear the wheel. Learn from every hit.

Adaptation Wheel adds a craftable golden wheel and the Sword of Extermination to Minecraft Java 1.20.1. Wear the wheel to build resistance to the attacks and hazards you survive, including damage and effects from other mods.

How the wheel works

  • Four stages of protection. Go from 20% to 40%, 70%, and finally 100% against a source. Repeat hits speed up the active stage.
  • A fifth stage for bosses. Complete Adaptation to Existence to gain immunity and a one-hit counter against that boss.
  • World Slash. Unlock a horizontal cut through walls with the Sword of Extermination. It deals 300 HP of damage, has a 45-second cooldown, and leaves blocks intact by default.
  • Adapt beyond combat. Learn to cope with low FPS, darkness, overcrowded inventories, and more. Fully adapting to low FPS unlocks the bundled Adapt Boost optimizer.

In-game controls

Craft the wheel with eight gold ingots around an ender pearl. Right-click to wear it. Press J to see your progress, search adaptations, or choose from nine wheel looks. Press F8 for Adapt Boost once unlocked.

Installation

Use Minecraft Java 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.0 or newer, and Java 17. Place one Adaptation Wheel JAR in mods. For multiplayer, use matching versions on the server and all clients. This download includes the prepared knowledge base and runs offline. No additional mod is required.
